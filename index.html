About Me

I am a Ph.D. researcher at Peking University, advised by Prof. Yun-Dong Wu. My research combines molecular simulation and machine learning to understand and design cyclic peptides and other bioactive molecules.

My interests include de novo peptide binder design, cyclic peptide structure prediction, molecular dynamics, and computational drug discovery. Previously, I studied computational chemistry at Southern University of Science and Technology.

I welcome opportunities for academic discussion and collaboration: daibt_97@stu.pku.edu.cn.

Research Interests

Peptide binder designCyclic peptidesMolecular dynamicsAI for drug discovery

De novo design of masking peptides

Developing computational approaches for disulfide-constrained peptide binders for conditionally activatable antibody prodrugs.

Conformational sampling and permeability

Using molecular dynamics and computational analysis to study cyclic peptide structures and the permeability of polar macrobicycles.
